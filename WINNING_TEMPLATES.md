# Qualtrics Winning Email Templates
### Ready-to-send templates for all 5 frameworks — built from the literal language of 382 emails that booked meetings

> Companion to `EMAIL_FRAMEWORKS.md`. That file explains *why* the frameworks work.
> **This file gives you the templates to copy, fill, and send.** Every sentence below is modeled on
> phrasing that recurred across the winning emails — not invented. Fill the `[brackets]` and go.

---

## The common threads (true in nearly every winner)

These six moves showed up across *all five* frameworks. They are the reason the templates are built
the way they are:

1. **Open by naming the relationship, not yourself.** The top openers were *"I'm on the Qualtrics
   team supporting [Company]"* / *"I support the [Company] + Qualtrics relationship alongside
   [Colleague]"* / *"Reaching out on behalf of the [Company] account team."* The most-repeated phrase
   in the whole dataset is **"account team here at Qualtrics."**
2. **One specific, true detail in line 1** — a role, a prior conversation, a trigger, a new-role
   congrats, or a referrer. Never "I hope this email finds you well."
3. **Proof through named peers.** *"…your peers including companies like Citi, AMEX, and JPMC"* /
   *"…life sciences orgs such as Boston Scientific, Johnson & Johnson and Roche."*
4. **A "reason to talk *now*"** — almost always **"what's new" (AI / new capabilities in the last 12
   months)** or a deadline (renewal, RFP, license sunset).
5. **One small, specific, soft ask.** The single most common CTA in the data, by a wide margin:
   **"Would you be open to a [20]-minute introductory call sometime next week?"**
6. **Under ~130 words.** 60% of winners land in the 76–130 word band.

## Framework 1 — "You Already Use Us" (Warm Expansion)
*Use when the account is already a Qualtrics customer and you're reaching a new stakeholder or team.*

### Template 1A — New stakeholder intro
```
Subject: Welcome to [Company] / Qualtrics partnership

Hi [First name], reaching out on behalf of the [Company] account team here at Qualtrics. You may
already be using our technology today — [Company] partners with us for a variety of [customer
experience / market research] use cases. We work closely with [internal license owner(s)], who own
the current license.

We've added some exciting new AI capabilities in the past 12 months that global orgs are just
beginning to deploy. I'd love to walk you through what's already in [Company]'s license and share
what's new on our roadmap.

Would you be open to a quick 20-minute intro sometime next week?

Best,
[Your name]
```
**📨 Real example that booked the meeting** — *Foodservice · "Welcome to Shamrock / Qualtrics partnership"*
> Hi Sam, great to meet you! Reaching out since I work on the Shamrock Foods account team here at
> Qualtrics. Our technology is being used for a variety of market research use cases at Shamrock
> today. We work primarily with Ann Ocana and Sandy Kelly, who are the current license owners.
>
> Would love to make introductions, give you some details about the current capabilities, and share
> some details about our new Digital Customer Experience platform.
>
> Would you be open to this?
>
> Best, Nick

> 💡 *Why it works:* names the **real license owners** (Ann Ocana, Sandy Kelly) as the credibility
> bridge, and the "what's new" is a **specific product** (Digital CX platform), not a vague claim.

## Framework 2 — "Reconnect / New Point of Contact" (Re-engagement)
*Use when there's a prior conversation, a lapsed relationship, or the account changed reps.*
**Highest reply rate in the dataset.**

### Template 2A — The classic realign (the single most-replied pattern)
```
Subject: Reconnecting — [Company] <> Qualtrics

Hi [First name], great to e-meet you! My name is [Your name] and I work alongside [Colleague] here
at Qualtrics.

Based on my notes, it looks like you connected with [Prior rep] back in [year/season] regarding
[specific project — e.g. exit-interview surveys / HCAHPS key drivers / pricing]. I wanted to
re-introduce [Colleague] and me as your new points of contact.

Qualtrics has made a number of changes since then — including new AI capabilities — so I thought it
would be beneficial to reconnect, see where things stand today, and realign on your [2026] priorities.

Would you have 20 minutes end of this week or early next so we can realign?

Best,
[Your name]
```
**📨 Real example that booked the meeting** — *Life Sciences (Elanco) · replied*
> Great to e-meet you! My name is Sara and I work alongside Colin Bowler here at Qualtrics. Based on
> my notes, it looks like you had connected with Todd Brown beginning of 2024 regarding a number of
> projects with Qualtrics — including a closed-loop action system and serving key customer insights
> to call-center agents. At the time, we were looking to connect with the CX team to ensure alignment
> on their initiatives.
>
> Qualtrics has made a number of changes since 2024, and I thought it would be beneficial for us to
> reconnect with the Elanco team and see where things are today. Would you have 20-minutes end of this
> week / early next so we can realign? — All the best, Sara

> 💡 *Why it works:* the **specific recalled detail** ("closed-loop action system… call-center agents,
> beginning of 2024") proves real homework — that specificity is what earns the reply.

## Framework 3 — "Role + Peer Proof" (Credible Cold Outreach)
*Use when it's genuinely cold/net-new, but you know their role and a relevant named peer.*

### Template 3A — Role relevance + named peers
```
Subject: [their domain] alignment   (e.g. "member insights program")

Hi [First name],

After seeing your role as [title] at [Company] and your background in [domain], I wanted to reach out
about [the specific outcome they own — e.g. enhancing your member feedback and insights program].

Not sure if you're familiar with Qualtrics, but we're helping similar [industry] organizations — like
[Named peer 1] and [Named peer 2] — [achieve specific outcome: analyze feedback across calls, chats,
and reviews; build a holistic view of the journey; and pinpoint where and when [customers] fall off].
We pair AI-powered automation with Natural Language Processing to get there.

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute introductory call in the coming weeks to compare approaches?

Best,
[Your name]
```
**📨 Real example that booked the meeting** — *FSI (Credit Union) · "member insights program"*
> Hi Melissa, after seeing your role at Homeowners Advantage and your expertise in mortgage
> experience, I wanted to reach out regarding the ability to enhance your member feedback and
> insights program.
>
> Not sure if you are familiar with Qualtrics, but we're working with similar credit unions, like
> Municipal, to help them achieve member-experience goals such as recording and analyzing member
> feedback, website and mobile optimization, and developing a holistic view of the member journey.
> We pair our AI-powered automation with Natural Language Processing to understand where and when
> members fall off.
>
> If you're available, I'd like to connect in April for brief introductions. Do you have availability
> in the coming weeks? — Best, Jack

> 💡 *Why it works:* the peer (**Municipal**, a fellow credit union) is an exact-match proof point, and
> the value is phrased as **their goals** ("member journey," "where members fall off"), not features.

## Framework 4 — "Trigger Strike" (Intent / Signal-Based)
*Use when they did something — demo view, content view, pricing request, new role, published
initiative, or inbound request. Keep it short (these were the briefest winners).*

```
Subject: Qualtrics — following up

Hi [First name], just gave you a call. I saw that you [watched one of our demo videos / requested
pricing on [product] / looked into some of our content], so I wanted to introduce myself as your
resource and POC here at Qualtrics.

I'd love to set up time to make introductions, better understand your interest, and speak to
Qualtrics in that context. Do you have 15–20 minutes this week or next? Feel free to grab a time on
my Calendly whenever works.

Best,
[Your name]
```
**📨 Real example that booked the meeting** — *Life Sciences (Novo) · "Intent – Demo Video View" · replied*
> Hi Lisa, just gave you a call. I saw that you watched one of our demo videos, and wanted to
> introduce myself as an available resource/POC for you. I'd love to set up some time to make
> introductions, better understand your interest, and speak to Qualtrics in that context. Do you have
> 20 minutes this week or next? Can't wait to meet! — Sara

> 💡 *Why it works:* it's **short, fast, and reactive** — references the exact action they took
> (watched a demo) and positions the rep as a helpful POC, not a pitch. Note the **phone call first.**

## Framework 5 — "Borrowed Trust" (Referral / Internal Sponsor / On-site)
*Use when someone can vouch for you, or your AE will be in their area.*

### Template 5A — Referral / internal sponsor
```
Subject: [Referrer]'s suggestion

Hi [First name], I'm reaching out at the suggestion of [Referrer + title], who recommended I connect
with you regarding [topic] — they felt it would be valuable for [team / strategic goal].

[One line of relevance: We help [peer type] like [Peer 1] and [Peer 2] [outcome].]

Would you be available for a brief 15–20 minute meeting the week of [date] to explore whether this
could support [their goal]?

Thank you,
[Your name]
```
**📨 Real example that booked the meeting** — *Government (SLG) · "Referral – County Manager"*
> Hey Jason, I'm reaching out at the suggestion of David Ellis, the County Manager. He recommended I

> a valuable asset for your strategic goals? Thank you!

> 💡 *Why it works:* the entire email rides on **one borrowed name** (the County Manager). When a
> trusted internal figure sends you, you barely need a value prop — just name them and ask.
