# Qualtrics Winning Email Templates
### Ready-to-send templates for all 5 frameworks — built from the literal language of 382 emails that booked meetings

> Companion to `EMAIL_FRAMEWORKS.md`. That file explains *why* the frameworks work.
> **This file gives you the templates to copy, fill, and send.** Every sentence below is modeled on
> phrasing that recurred across the winning emails — not invented. Fill the `[brackets]` and go.

---

## The common threads (true in nearly every winner)

These six moves showed up across *all five* frameworks. They are the reason the templates are built
the way they are:

1. **Open by naming the relationship, not yourself.** The top openers were *"I'm on the Qualtrics
   team supporting [Company]"* / *"I support the [Company] + Qualtrics relationship alongside
   [Colleague]"* / *"Reaching out on behalf of the [Company] account team."* The most-repeated phrase
   in the whole dataset is **"account team here at Qualtrics."**
2. **One specific, true detail in line 1** — a role, a prior conversation, a trigger, a new-role
   congrats, or a referrer. Never "I hope this email finds you well."
3. **Proof through named peers.** *"…your peers including companies like Citi, AMEX, and JPMC"* /
   *"…life sciences orgs such as Boston Scientific, Johnson & Johnson and Roche."*
4. **A "reason to talk *now*"** — almost always **"what's new" (AI / new capabilities in the last 12
   months)** or a deadline (renewal, RFP, license sunset).
5. **One small, specific, soft ask.** The single most common CTA in the data, by a wide margin:
   **"Would you be open to a [20]-minute introductory call sometime next week?"**
6. **Under ~130 words.** 60% of winners land in the 76–130 word band.

## Framework 1 — "You Already Use Us" (Warm Expansion)
*Use when the account is already a Qualtrics customer and you're reaching a new stakeholder or team.*

### Template 1A — New stakeholder intro
```
Subject: Welcome to [Company] / Qualtrics partnership

Hi [First name], reaching out on behalf of the [Company] account team here at Qualtrics. You may
already be using our technology today — [Company] partners with us for a variety of [customer
experience / market research] use cases. We work closely with [internal license owner(s)], who own
the current license.

We've added some exciting new AI capabilities in the past 12 months that global orgs are just
beginning to deploy. I'd love to walk you through what's already in [Company]'s license and share
what's new on our roadmap.

Would you be open to a quick 20-minute intro sometime next week?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 1B — Cross-department expansion ("are you aware?")
```
Subject: Consolidation

Hi [First name], [Your name] here — wanted to ask if you were aware that [Company] is already a
Qualtrics customer? We work closely with your [customer experience / research] team, who leverage
us to collect and act on feedback.

I'm reaching out to you specifically because I understand [product/employee experience] falls under
your purview, and we've added some advanced AI functionality I figured you'd want to take a look at.

Are you open to setting aside 30 minutes next week to see what that looks like?

[Your name]
```

### Template 1C — Renewal + expansion / new account director
```
Subject: Qualtrics Current Partnership

Hi [First name], I'm the new account director on the [Company] team here at Qualtrics and wanted to
introduce myself. I'd love to learn how things are going with your current license and align on your
upcoming renewal.

We've also added some exciting new [AI / qualitative] capabilities in the past 12 months that teams
like yours are just beginning to deploy — happy to share those as well.

Are you available for 30 minutes Thursday or Friday to connect?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Framework 2 — "Reconnect / New Point of Contact" (Re-engagement)
*Use when there's a prior conversation, a lapsed relationship, or the account changed reps.*
**Highest reply rate in the dataset.**

### Template 2A — The classic realign (the single most-replied pattern)
```
Subject: Reconnecting — [Company] <> Qualtrics

Hi [First name], great to e-meet you! My name is [Your name] and I work alongside [Colleague] here
at Qualtrics.

Based on my notes, it looks like you connected with [Prior rep] back in [year/season] regarding
[specific project — e.g. exit-interview surveys / HCAHPS key drivers / pricing]. I wanted to
re-introduce [Colleague] and me as your new points of contact.

Qualtrics has made a number of changes since then — including new AI capabilities — so I thought it
would be beneficial to reconnect, see where things stand today, and realign on your [2026] priorities.

Would you have 20 minutes end of this week or early next so we can realign?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 2B — Prior rep has left
```
Subject: [Company] <> Qualtrics — new point of contact

Hi [First name], we haven't had the chance to meet yet, but I wanted to reach out regarding some
conversations you had earlier with [Prior rep]. [Prior rep] is unfortunately no longer with
Qualtrics, so I'd like to introduce [Colleague] and me as your new points of contact.

We'd love to update you on our real-time [patient experience and CAHPS / employee listening]
capabilities. Could we grab a few minutes on the calendar to reconnect — maybe sometime [next week]?

[Your name]
```

### Template 2C — Reconnect + deadline (RFP / renewal)
```
Subject: Reconnecting before [the RFP / renewal]

Hi [First name], I hope the year's off to a great start! I support [Colleague], who you met with in
[season/year]. With [the possibility of an RFP / your renewal] coming up, it felt like the right time
to reconnect and catch up.

We've had quite a few changes — both pricing and innovation-wise — and would love to learn more about
your strategy this year.

Do you have 15 minutes to reconnect in the coming week or two?

Best,
[Your name]
```
> **Pro move (proven in the data):** pair 2A/2B with a **same-day voicemail** — *"Hi [Name], just
> left you a voicemail…"* The call + email one-two punch recurs throughout the replied-tagged winners.

## Framework 3 — "Role + Peer Proof" (Credible Cold Outreach)
*Use when it's genuinely cold/net-new, but you know their role and a relevant named peer.*

### Template 3A — Role relevance + named peers
```
Subject: [their domain] alignment   (e.g. "member insights program")

Hi [First name],

After seeing your role as [title] at [Company] and your background in [domain], I wanted to reach out
about [the specific outcome they own — e.g. enhancing your member feedback and insights program].

Not sure if you're familiar with Qualtrics, but we're helping similar [industry] organizations — like
[Named peer 1] and [Named peer 2] — [achieve specific outcome: analyze feedback across calls, chats,
and reviews; build a holistic view of the journey; and pinpoint where and when [customers] fall off].
We pair AI-powered automation with Natural Language Processing to get there.

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute introductory call in the coming weeks to compare approaches?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 3B — "By way of introduction" + peer cohort
```
Subject: [Company] contact center initiatives

[First name],

By way of introduction, my name is [Your name] and I work on the [Company] account team here at
Qualtrics. Given your role leading [area], I believe it'd be beneficial to connect.

We'd love to review how many of your peers — including companies like [Peer 1], [Peer 2], and
[Peer 3] — have used our platform to [ingest, analyze, and act on customer interactions across calls,
chats, emails, surveys, and reviews], along with the significant cost savings they're finding.

Please let me know if you or one of your directs has availability for a brief introductory call in
the coming weeks.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 3C — Competitor displacement / benchmark
```
Subject: cx platform comparison

Hi [First name], it's possible you've had some exposure to Qualtrics over the past few years. We
understand [Company] currently leverages [Competitor] for your CX program. Is now a good time to
benchmark what you're doing today vs. what the future could look like?

I'd like to connect you with the [Company] account head to review the conversations we're having in
other departments, along with the advances we've made in AI, NLP, and omnichannel CX.

Is there a time that works to connect over the next couple weeks?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Framework 4 — "Trigger Strike" (Intent / Signal-Based)
*Use when they did something — demo view, content view, pricing request, new role, published
initiative, or inbound request. Keep it short (these were the briefest winners).*

### Template 4A — Intent signal (demo / content / pricing)
```
Subject: Qualtrics — following up

Hi [First name], just gave you a call. I saw that you [watched one of our demo videos / requested
pricing on [product] / looked into some of our content], so I wanted to introduce myself as your
resource and POC here at Qualtrics.

I'd love to set up time to make introductions, better understand your interest, and speak to
Qualtrics in that context. Do you have 15–20 minutes this week or next? Feel free to grab a time on
my Calendly whenever works.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 4B — New-role congrats
```
Subject: future alignment with [Company]

Hi [First name], major congrats on the new role at [Company] — exciting to see! I know you'd had some
conversations with Qualtrics a while back, and figured now might be a good time to reconnect.

We're working with [peer leaders / similar teams] to [outcome — e.g. get closer to the customer
experience using real-time feedback]. As you look to make an immediate impact, we'd love to share how
we can be a resource.

Would you be open to a quick intro this week or next?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 4C — Public initiative / report trigger
```
Subject: [their published initiative]

Hi [First name], I really enjoyed reading about [your approach to [topic] in the recent [article] /
the [Report Name] from [month/year]]. [Restate their stated priority — e.g. understanding the drivers
of burnout and turnover is paramount for [their goal]].

Qualtrics helps [peer type] gain continuous, actionable insight into [the experience], so you can
proactively identify pain points before they grow. We've seen this [reduce call volumes by 23% / cut
research timelines from months to days] by surfacing root causes hidden in siloed data.

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute chat this week or next to explore how this could support
[their goal]?

Best,
[Your name]
```

### Template 4D — Inbound contact request (fastest yes)
```
Hi [First name], just gave you a call. I received your request for contact and wanted to introduce
myself as your available resource/POC. I'd love to set up time to discuss what you mentioned in your
notes. Do you have 15–20 minutes this week or next? Feel free to grab a time on my Calendly whenever
works — looking forward to it!

[Your name]
```

## Framework 5 — "Borrowed Trust" (Referral / Internal Sponsor / On-site)
*Use when someone can vouch for you, or your AE will be in their area.*

### Template 5A — Referral / internal sponsor
```
Subject: [Referrer]'s suggestion

Hi [First name], I'm reaching out at the suggestion of [Referrer + title], who recommended I connect
with you regarding [topic] — they felt it would be valuable for [team / strategic goal].

[One line of relevance: We help [peer type] like [Peer 1] and [Peer 2] [outcome].]

Would you be available for a brief 15–20 minute meeting the week of [date] to explore whether this
could support [their goal]?

Thank you,
[Your name]
```

### Template 5B — On-site / AE in town (adds scarcity)
```
Subject: In [City] next week

Hi [First name], my name is [Your name] and I work for Qualtrics. [Peer orgs — e.g. agencies in the
State of Texas, Harris County, and the City of Houston] currently partner with us to improve
[resident, customer, and employee experiences].

My account executive [AE name] will be in [City] next week meeting with a few of our current clients.
Given [Company]'s work on [initiative], are you available for them to stop by for 20 minutes to
introduce themselves?

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

### Template 5C — Colleague-sourced insight (warm signal from inside)
```
Subject: [Colleague]'s conversations <> Qualtrics

Hi [First name], great to meet you! My colleague [Colleague] has been hearing from [Company]'s
[team — e.g. Research Operations] that there could be interest in [topic — e.g. our AI text-analytics
capabilities].

Our AI helps decrease the time spent taking action — with recommended responses to customer emails,
texts, chats, and reviews. I'd love to share how it's being used and where it could fit for your team.

Would you be open to connecting in the coming weeks?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## 30-second send checklist
- [ ] Line 1 names the **relationship + one specific true detail** (no "hope this finds you well").
- [ ] Exactly **one value idea**, ideally with a **named peer** from the proof bank.
- [ ] A **"why now"** — what's new (AI) or a deadline.
- [ ] CTA is a **small, specific, soft time ask** ending in a question.
- [ ] **Under ~130 words.** No feature dump, no pricing, no attachment. The only goal is the meeting.
